SnapFly Capture Support

Official help and contact information for SnapFly Capture on macOS.

Contact Support

For questions, bug reports, purchase or restore issues, and feature feedback, email the developer directly.

Email SnapFly Support

Support email: cem.kirac@gmail.com

What to Include

  • Your macOS version and Mac model.
  • The SnapFly Capture version shown in Settings.
  • The steps that caused the issue and what you expected to happen.
  • A screenshot of the issue when appropriate. Do not include sensitive information.

Common Help

Screen capture permission: Open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen & System Audio Recording and allow SnapFly Capture.

Save folder access: Choose the image or video save folder in SnapFly Settings. macOS may ask you to confirm access to that folder.

Restore Pro: Open SnapFly Capture Pro and choose Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account used for the purchase.

Privacy

SnapFly Capture processes screenshots, recordings, OCR results, and history locally on your Mac. Support requests only include information you voluntarily send by email.
